**Addressing Leftovers**

**Hidden Items**  
The primary functional deficiency lies in this area, rather than the labels.

Version 1.7 addressed disambiguation; however, the Hide/Unhide functions fail to preserve playback. The _hide_current method reduces self.images without clearing _shuffle_order, which may lead to IndexErrors. The Unhide function reconstructs the list without invoking _show_current(), resulting in empty sources displaying “No images left.” Additionally, hiding the desktop image does not trigger a replacement.

Implement the _rebuild_playback_after_filter_change function to reset images and the shuffle deck, activated by Hide, Unhide, and editor Save actions. If the hidden path pertains to the wallpaper, apply the new image silently or retain the previous one if no new image is available. Orphan purges should be executed explicitly and off-thread; automatic purging during startup may lead to curation loss in instances of NAS downtime. Verify if the folder is online from the cache, rather than during dialogue reconstruction.

**Interactive Per-Display Map**  
Maintain the current block.

The topology canvas and COM picker represent separate identity spaces. Merging them by ordinal presents challenges. A clickable map prior to fingerprinting may produce inaccurate outputs. Joining sessions based on maximum RECT intersection is optimal. Persistent identity should be based on EDID hash, size, origin, primary, and orientation, reconciled during WM_DISPLAYCHANGE/resume/dock events—never persist the COM path. Version 1.8 should enhance read-only labels and identify unresolved displays; Phase E is designated for version 2.0.

**Narrator**  
Refrain from simulation.

CustomTkinter is built on Tk and does not feature a complete UIA tree. A toast-based “Narrator mode” would misrepresent the functionality. Version 1.8 can enhance non-AT capabilities: adjust tab order, ensure no focus interruptions on grid_removed pages, provide text equivalents for icon-only controls, respect SPI_GETHIGHCONTRAST, and output per-monitor DPI from --selftest. Include a genuine Narrator script in VERIFICATION.md for hardware testing. Document the limitations of the toolkit as failures, rather than as misleading providers.
